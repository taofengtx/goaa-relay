### Step 1: 刪除 Testimonials Section (致命優先)

**找位置**:
- 在 Layers panel 找名稱包含 `testimonials` / `reviews` / `quotes` 的 section
- 或者用 ⌘+F (Mac) / Ctrl+F (Win) 搜尋「Radison」

**識別特徵** (Batch 1 真實確認):
```
"Radison transformed our workflow with incredible AI solutions. 
 Their expertise truly exceeded all expectations!"

"Radison provided game-changing insights that helped us optimize 
 processes and scale operations fast."

"Radison's AI tools revolutionized how we work, saving time and 
 driving our productivity forward."
```

**動作**:
1. 點選整個 testimonials section (Layers panel 點)

> - 目前 GOAA 沒有真實 user testimonial (規範 SUPREME 1.4 Runtime Truth)
